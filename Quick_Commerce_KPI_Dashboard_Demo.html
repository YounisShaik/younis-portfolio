Key Insights

What this dashboard is telling a growth/ops lead, in plain terms
  • Growth is healthy, not just big. GMV and orders are both trending up, and delivery time and on-time % are improving in parallel — scaling usually breaks ops, but that isn't happening here.
  • Retention is being earned, not bought. Discount burn (% of GMV) is trending down while CSAT and repeat order rate are trending up — customers are staying because the experience is better, not because of coupons.
  • Inventory is improving and it's paying off. Falling stockout rate lines up with rising repeat rate — fewer "out of stock" moments is likely driving some of the retention gain.
  • Koramangala needs riders, not marketing. It has the highest order volume but is flagged "Needs Intervention" — delivery time is over target despite strong repeat rate, pointing to a capacity problem, not a demand problem.
  • HSR Layout and Whitefield need inventory attention — both show elevated stockout rates that are likely capping their repeat-rate upside.
