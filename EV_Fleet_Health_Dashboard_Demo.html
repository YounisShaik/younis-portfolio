Key Insights

What this dashboard is telling an EV product/engineering lead, in plain terms
  • Degradation rate, not SoH, is the number to watch first. A battery can look fine on SoH today but be declining fast — the monthly rate predicts trouble before SoH itself looks bad.
  • Eco mode nearly matches the rated spec, Sport mode doesn't come close. In Bangalore city driving, Eco delivers close to the rated 120 km thanks to conservative power draw and stop-go regen — Sport mode's aggressive acceleration cuts that by roughly 40%, so range claims need to be mode-specific, not a single number.
  • P1 faults are rare but that's exactly why they need a dedicated view — buried inside a general fault count, a handful of critical safety issues would go unnoticed next to hundreds of minor ones.
  • Test pass rate is climbing ahead of falling warranty claims — a sign that upstream QC improvements are already reducing downstream cost, a few weeks before it fully shows up in claims data.
